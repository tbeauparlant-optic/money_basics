Understanding Loans: Interest Adds to the Total Cost

This example shows how the total cost of the same loan can change when the interest rate changes.

Review each example and notice how a higher interest rate increases the total amount paid over time.

Base Loan Amount

$10,000

Examples

Example 1

Interest rate: 9.0%

  • Potential annual loan interest $900
  • Actual total interest $4,500
  • Actual total loan and interest $14,500

Example 2

Interest rate: 12.0%

  • Potential annual loan interest $1,200
  • Actual total interest $6,000
  • Actual total loan and interest $16,000

Example 3

Interest rate: 15.0%

  • Potential annual loan interest $1,500
  • Actual total interest $7,500
  • Actual total loan and interest $17,500

Takeaway

Higher-interest loans can cost much more over time, even when the original loan amount stays the same.
