Pros and Cons of Credit

Think about your own experience with credit, along with the examples and ideas in the Student Guide.

Before completing this activity, review Student Guide pages 105–107.

As you read, notice examples of how credit can be useful, as well as examples of how it can become expensive, stressful, or hard to manage.

Then use the space below to create your own list of pros and cons of credit.

There are no right or wrong answers here. The goal is to reflect on what stands out to you from the reading and from your own experience.

Instructions

Add your own examples of possible pros and cons of credit.

  • your reading of Student Guide pages 105–107
  • your own experience
  • examples you have seen in everyday life

When you are done, you can download your list for later reference.
