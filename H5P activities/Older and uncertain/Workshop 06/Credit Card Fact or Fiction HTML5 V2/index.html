Welcome

In this activity, you'll review a few important credit terms and then decide whether each statement is Fact or Fiction. The goal is to build understanding, not to trick you. After each answer, you'll see a short explanation.

There is no timer. Move at a pace that works for you.

Before We Begin: How Credit Works

Credit activity is recorded by lenders and can appear on your credit reports.

That information helps shape your credit scores over time.

Paying bills on time and using credit carefully can help you build healthier credit habits.
